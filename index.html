About the Station

Gas & Grill, done right.

VP Racing Fuels is a gas station and convenience store serving Thomasville and the surrounding area since January 2020. Stop in for VP Racing fuel, diesel, and everything you need from the store — all in a wheelchair‑accessible building with onsite services.

We're a straightforward, dependable stop for locals and travelers passing through on US‑84 — no car wash, no oil changes, just fuel, a full convenience store, and fast service.
